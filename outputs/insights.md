# Key Insights — Personal Expense Tracker Analysis

> This project uses synthetic practice data generated with NumPy. The patterns below are for learning the workflow only; they are not real financial records.

- Dataset size: **420 transactions** across 2025, total spending **579,051.71 BDT**.
- The highest spending month was **April** with **59,075.07 BDT** in expenses.
- The top spending category is **Food**, taking **34.14%** of all spending.
- Average monthly savings rate: **13.51%** of income (income modelled as 55,000 BDT/month plus small variation).
- Essential spending (Food, Transport, Rent, Bills, Health, Education) makes up **91.99%** of total spending.
